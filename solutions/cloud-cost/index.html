Cloud Cost Optimization

A cloud cost solution that lowers spend by watching usage and resizing or removing what you do not need. Monitoring and optimization, aimed at the resources themselves.

Core features

Usage and spend

Shows which servers, disks, and services are costing money.

Idle resources

Finds instances, storage, and addresses that are paid for but barely used.

Right-size

Points at a smaller size when a resource has spare capacity.

Waste alerts

Notifies you when usage or spend drifts from the plan.

Before and after

Shows how usage changed after a resize or a shutdown.

Keep capacity

Suggests cuts that leave the capacity the service still needs.
