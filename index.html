AsleepRemote

Turn your phone into a Bluetooth touchpad, keyboard, and air mouse for your PC.

Get it on Google Play

The app

AsleepRemote lets you control your PC from your phone, the way a TV remote controls a television. The phone connects over Bluetooth as a keyboard and mouse, so there is nothing to install on the PC. Pair it in your PC's Bluetooth settings and you can start.

Touchpad

Move the cursor, tap to click, scroll with two fingers, and use left, right, and middle buttons.

Air mouse

Point and move the cursor with your phone's motion sensors.

Keyboard

Type on your PC with the keyboard on your phone.

Media remote

Play, pause, skip, go back, mute, and change the volume.

Shortcuts

Build your own Ctrl, Alt, Shift, and Windows key combinations.

Requirements

Android 9 or higher with Bluetooth HID, and a Bluetooth PC. Windows is recommended.
