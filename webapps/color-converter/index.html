Color Converter

Change any value. The other formats and the swatch stay in step.

How to use it

HEX accepts 3 or 6 digits, with or without #. RGB channels run from 0 to 255. H is the hue in degrees, and S and L are saturation and lightness as percents.

Nothing is sent to a server.
