Screenshots

The app

HueSkin estimates skin type and personal color on the device. Photos are not uploaded. They stay on this phone. The report is a style reference, not a medical diagnosis.

Skin analysis

Oil and water balance, calmness, wrinkles, elasticity, and even tone. A region heatmap and a short care routine sit with the score. Face-shape notes are included as a reference.

Personal color

Warm, cool, or neutral undertone, plus a spring, summer, autumn, or winter feeling. The report names a 12-season type, shows best and worst palettes, and previews virtual draping.

Trends and history

Scores over time, and past checks, stay on the phone so you can see what changed.

On the phone

Analysis works offline. Use the front or back camera, or pick a photo from the gallery. The app includes 19 languages.

Ads

A banner may appear on Home and on reports. A one-time purchase in Settings removes ads. You can also watch an optional ad to support HueSkin. Analysis still works offline.

Not a diagnosis

Scores and color notes are for reference only. They are not a medical diagnosis and not a substitute for a clinician.
