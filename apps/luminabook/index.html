Quotes and answers for Android

Lumina Book

A quiet place for a tired day. Open it for a warm quote, or bring a question to the book of answers. The library stays on your phone. Now on Google Play.

Get it on Google Play

Screenshots

The app

Daily quotes include the line, a short note about who said it, and a gentle reading. Swipe sideways for another line. The book of answers is comfort, not a prediction and not professional advice.

Quotes

Read today's line, then swipe for another. Open the story when you want the longer reading. Save the lines you want to keep.

Book of answers

The book stays closed until you are ready. Think of a question, then touch the book or give the phone a light shake.

Share, widget, reminder

Share a card with the Lumina Book name along the bottom. A home screen widget shows today's line without opening the app. You choose the hour for a daily reminder. The reminder stays on your phone.

On the phone

The library is inside the app, so the quotes and answers work offline. Settings lists 26 languages, and the app can follow the phone. Light and dark themes are both included.

Quiet piano

A public-domain piano recording of Satie's Gymnopédie No. 1, from Musopen, can play while the app is open. It stays off until you turn it on.

Ads

A banner sits at the top. A full-screen ad may appear after every ten quotes or ten answers. Buy me a coffee is a short ad you choose to watch, not a payment. A one-time USD 1.00 purchase removes the ads.

Lumina Book does not give medical, legal, or financial advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.

Free on Google Play, with ads and a one-time in-app purchase. Rated Everyone. Google Play · Privacy policy
