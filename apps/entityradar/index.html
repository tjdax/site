EntityRadar

Turn the magnetic field, signal strength, and sound already on your phone into a science-fiction radar. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account. Now on Google Play.

Screenshots

The app

EntityRadar is an offline entertainment app. It reads sensors already on the phone and draws them like a radar. The radar runs only while the app is on screen. Leaving the app stops the sensors. It visualizes those sensors. It does not detect supernatural entities.

EMF pulse

The compass sensor shows the magnetic field in microteslas. A steady everyday field keeps the radar green. A sudden jump turns the screen yellow, then red, and speeds up a beep.

Signal interferometer

The connected Wi-Fi strength and, if you allow it, the cellular signal strength are drawn as a live oscilloscope. A sharp drop adds a short TV-static glitch.

EVP spectrum

The microphone becomes frequency bars while the screen is open. Audio is not saved. If you deny the microphone, the bars stay locked.

Camera

The camera stays off until you turn it on. If a person is visible, the app draws a stick figure on the phone. Joint positions are not saved and are not uploaded. Snap saves one photo, with the radar overlay, to the gallery.

Ads

A banner sits under the radar until you buy High-performance sensor mode, a one-time purchase that removes it. An optional video in Settings can unlock a hidden color theme.

On the phone

There is no account and no app server. Sensor readings stay on the device. The app includes a language picker and falls back to English when a translation is missing.

Free on Google Play, with ads and a one-time in-app purchase. Google Play · Privacy policy
